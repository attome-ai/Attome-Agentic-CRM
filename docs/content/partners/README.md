# Partner programme

Attome partners implement, host and extend Attome for their own customers. Joining is free; tiers are
earned by certified people, delivered projects and customer satisfaction, never bought.

## Partner types

- **Implementation partners** design, build, migrate and roll out Attome for a customer, using the
  [methodology](methodology.md).
- **Hosting partners** run Attome for their customers, each customer on its own deployment and database,
  under the partner's own name (see [Trademark](#trademark)).
- **App builders** publish packs, connectors and apps built on the public API and SDK.

A partner can be more than one type.

## Tiers

| Tier | Earned by | Gets |
|---|---|---|
| Registered | Signing the partner agreement. No fee. | The partner portal, deal registration, the delivery toolkit and training material. |
| Certified | Certified people and delivered projects | Everything above, leads from Attome and a listing in the public partner directory. |
| Premier | More certified people, more delivered projects and high customer satisfaction | Everything above, priority leads, co-selling, early access to releases, and a share of support and subscription revenue on customers the partner brings. |

Tiers are reviewed every quarter against the partner's certified people, delivered projects and customer
satisfaction. The numbers each tier needs, and the revenue terms, are in the partner agreement.

## What a partner commits to

- **Certified people.** Projects are led by people who hold the Attome certification for their role
  (administrator, builder, developer or consultant), and certifications are kept current.
- **The methodology.** Projects follow the [implementation methodology](methodology.md), so every customer
  gets the same checkpoints: changesets reviewed before they reach production, customer tests that pass,
  backups proven by a restore.
- **Customer data stays with the customer.** A partner never copies customer data into its own systems,
  never mixes two customers' data, and never puts customer data in a public issue, a forum post or a
  support ticket attachment it does not need.
- **Security problems are reported privately**, through [SECURITY.md](../../../SECURITY.md), never in public.
- **Support as the first line.** A partner that sells support answers its customers first and escalates to
  Attome as the second line, with the evidence the support guide asks for.
- **Quality checks.** Attome samples delivered projects (configuration, tests, upgrade readiness). A partner
  that fails them repeatedly loses its tier, and a person who misuses a certification loses it.

## Deal registration

A registered deal protects the partner that found it for the protection period in the partner agreement.
Attome checks a new registration against existing ones (names in Arabic and English included) and confirms
or refuses it. A confirmed deal is the partner's to work during that period.

## Trademark

Anyone may say they use, support or build on Attome. A hosting partner hosts under its own name and may not
present its service as Attome's own. A modified fork may not be called Attome or use the logo.

## Joining

Apply through the partner portal. Attome reviews the application, the partner signs the agreement, and the
partner's people start the certification paths. Recruitment starts in the Gulf and Egypt.
