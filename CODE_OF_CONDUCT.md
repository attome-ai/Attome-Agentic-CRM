# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the **Contributor Covenant, version 2.1**, available at
<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>.

In short: be respectful and welcoming, criticise ideas and not people, and do not harass anyone. This
applies in issues, discussions and every other project space.

Report unacceptable behaviour to **support@attome.com**. Reports are handled confidentially by the
project team, who may warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban anyone who breaks this code, as the
Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines describe.
