A capability pack is configuration that only ever GRANTS.

It cannot raise a session's risk ceiling, add a privilege, bypass the gate, or make an unlicensed module available. Referencing this pack from an agent policy adds its action ids to that policy's allow-list — and every other check still runs on every invocation.
